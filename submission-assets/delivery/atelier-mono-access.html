N° 02 First Steps Sheet 02 / 02

A slow, deliberate start.
Four steps, no more.

Step — 01
Remix the project

Follow the remix link on Sheet 01. Framer places a full copy in your workspace — nothing is shared, nothing is linked back.

Step — 02
Replace the work

Open the Projects CMS collection and swap in your own buildings, images, and case studies. The Selected Works grid, tags, and detail pages update themselves.

Step — 03
Write your manifesto

Section N° 01 opens the site with a statement, not a slogan. Say what your practice believes — then let the numbered sections carry the rest.

Step — 04
Publish

Connect your domain in Site Settings → Domains and publish. One typeface, a stone-and-ivory palette, and motion that whispers — your website now agrees with your point of view.
