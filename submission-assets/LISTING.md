## Long Description

**Architecture deserves a website that knows when to stay quiet.**

Atelier Mono is an editorial portfolio template for architects, interior studios, and spatial designers who let the work speak. Built around a strict numbered-section system — N° 01 through N° 10 — it reads less like a website and more like a beautifully bound studio monograph.

Every detail is deliberate: one typeface, a restrained stone-and-ivory palette, generous negative space, and motion that whispers instead of shouts.

**What's inside**

- **A numbered editorial system** — every section is indexed like the pages of a portfolio book, with hairline rules and sheet labels that give the whole site a printed, archival feel.
- **CMS-powered Selected Works** — a curated project grid with full case-study detail pages. Add a project in the CMS and the grid, tags, and detail pages update themselves.
- **A manifesto, not a mission statement** — an opening statement section that positions your practice before a single image appears.
- **Materials index** — an interactive accordion ("Three materials, at most") for the textures your studio builds with: stone, timber, glass, concrete.
- **A slow, deliberate process** — a dark, numbered process section that walks clients through how you work, step by step.
- **Recognition & Voices** — an awards table and a client-testimonial slider that earn trust without breaking the tone.
- **Field notes journal** — an editorial writing section for essays on drawing, light, and the parts of building that never make the photographs.
- **Begin a Project** — a full inquiry form on a dark closing section, so the site ends where new work begins.

**Design details**

- Full-screen index menu overlay with numbered navigation
- Scroll-triggered reveals and eased micro-interactions throughout
- Hover states on every project card, link, and control
- Fully responsive — considered layouts for desktop, tablet, and phone
- Built with clean, consistently named layers and reusable components (Buttons, Tags, Cards, Sliders, Accordions), so restyling the entire site takes minutes

**Who it's for**

Architecture practices, interior design studios, landscape architects, furniture designers, spatial and set designers, engineers of quiet buildings — and any creative who believes restraint is a feature.

Swap the images, write your manifesto, publish. Your practice already has a point of view — Atelier Mono makes sure your website agrees with it.
